As you return to the OS card pool—or discover it for the first time—and start "brewing" your deck, you will find yourself gravitating toward certain cards and builds. Why those, out of everything? OS means different things to different players, and it will be interesting to see where you end up.

**"Spikes"** craft top-tier decks and use precise play to win games, matches, and tournaments. If you're a Spike, you're testing repeatedly to dial in the perfect ratio of, say, Disenchant (p. [X](#disenchant)) to Divine Offering (p. [X](#divine-offering)). You drive the metagame forward, discovering optimizations before your competition. You challenge all of us to build smarter and play better. You think in terms of efficiency, mana curves, and card advantage.

Or maybe you're more into **spice**: the overlooked card or novel combo. Where some see a useless artifact, you see a surprise finisher. You're mining for undiscovered gems, and you're constantly seeking synergy. Your deck's aesthetics—not just visual, but conceptual—matter more than you'd admit. While you will win fewer matches and tournaments, your own personal sense of victory comes from your brew "doing its thing" once or twice. You need Spikes in your life to keep you honest: you still need a wincon!

You may also be here for the **nostalgia**. Craw Wurm (p. [X](#craw-wurm)) is neither efficient nor spicy, but you're still going to run a playset. For you, _Magic_ is about hearkening back to the floor of a tent, running four-color unsleeved decks with impossible mana bases for hours while flies buzz outside. A good piece of art may be all it takes to earn a slot in your list. While you may not win many competitive matches, you will win the admiration of Spikes and spicers alike.
