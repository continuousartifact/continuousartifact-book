_Magic_'s competitive play governing body does not define an official "Old School" format. Instead, various groups of enthusiasts from around the world have invented their own sets of OS rules.

The original OS ruleset, now known as "Swedish" (SWE), excluded the Fallen Empires set and restricted Mishra's Workshop (p. [X](#mishras-workshop)) and Strip Mine (p. [X](#strip-mine)). Atlantic (ATL), currently the most popular format in the US, allows Fallen Empires as well as 4 Workshops. Eternal Central (EC), Pacific (PAC), and Boreal (BOR) are "4 Strip" formats that allow a full playset of Strip Mines, leading to savage, fast-paced games; each has its own twist on other restrictions.

OS generally follows modern _Magic_ rules, with the notable exception of mana burn (see below). Ante-related cards are generally banned. Check the internet for other errata, like the rule governing Chaos Orb (p. [X](#chaos-orb)) flips. Also remember that the text on old cards is often ambiguous or even wrong: check Scryfall for current wording.
